A recent case from our clinic

One patient came to us with a loose upper denture that kept dropping when he talked and ate. Significant bone loss meant conventional implants were not possible.

Using zygomatic (cheek) implants, Dr George placed the implants without bone grafting, and the patient left with a fixed set of teeth on the same day. Dr Sunny Kotecha then fitted the final bridge. He now eats, speaks and smiles with confidence, with no removable denture.
