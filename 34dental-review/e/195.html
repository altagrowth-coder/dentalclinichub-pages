How much will it cost?

Your first visit has a fixed, upfront fee, and you receive your treatment options and costs in writing before anything goes ahead. Treatment can be spread over 12 months at 0% with Tabeo.

I feel nervous about the dentist

You are in good company. We take things at your pace, explain every step, and offer IV sedation in house for patients who would like extra help to relax.

What if I decide not to go ahead?

That is completely fine. Your visit is about giving you clear information and a plan. If you see our Treatment Coordinator, the £20 is credited to your treatment or refunded.

How quickly can I be seen?

We release new appointments every week. Booking online shows you every available time, so you can pick the one that suits you.
