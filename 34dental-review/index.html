How It Works

Each patient follows one journey at a time. Moving a lead in the system switches the journey automatically, so nobody gets the wrong message or two at once.

1. New enquiryWhatsApp and email within a minute, shaped to the treatment they asked about.
2. After the callA recap straight after the team speaks to them, then gentle reminders.
3. Not respondingA friendly nudge when the team cannot get through.
4. Booked or nurtureA welcome pack when they book, or a monthly check-in if they are not ready yet.
