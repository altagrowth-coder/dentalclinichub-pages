Questions patients ask us

How long does it take?

Simple cases can be a few months, more complex ones over a year. Your ClinCheck simulation gives you the number before you start.

Does it hurt?

There is pressure and tenderness for a day or two after each new aligner. Most patients describe it as noticeable rather than painful.

Can I eat normally?

Yes. The aligners come out to eat and drink, then go straight back in. You wear them around 22 hours a day.

Will anyone notice?

Rarely. They are clear and fit closely to your teeth.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Most patients spread the cost, and you see the monthly figure before you commit.
