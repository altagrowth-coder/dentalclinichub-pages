What happens on the day

  1. A warm welcomeOur reception team greets you and you meet the person looking after you.
  2. A relaxed conversationWe listen first. Tell us what you’d love to change about your smile, and anything that worries you.
  3. A thorough checkWe look after your teeth, gums and overall oral health, with x-rays only where they’re needed.
  4. Your personal planWe talk you through your options in plain English and give you a written plan with clear prices.

You decide everything in your own time. Larger treatment can be spread into monthly payments.
