Good to know

Is the video call really free?

Yes. Your video consultation is completely free and there’s no obligation to go ahead with anything.

Do I need to download anything?

No. You’ll receive a link by email. Tap it at your appointment time and the call opens on your phone or computer.

Can I keep my camera off?

Of course. Many patients prefer to just talk. If you’d like us to take a look at your smile, you can switch your camera on at any point.

We’ll give you a clear guide to costs and monthly payment options. Your exact treatment plan follows a clinical assessment at the practice.

Can someone join me?

Absolutely. Partners and family are very welcome on the call.
