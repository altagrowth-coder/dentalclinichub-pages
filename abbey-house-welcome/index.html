Before you arrive

Arrive a few minutes early

You will be asked to complete a short medical history questionnaire before you go into the surgery.

Bring your prescription list

If you have a repeat prescription, bring it with you so we have a full list of your medications.

Write your questions down

People forget them in the chair. Bring the list, we would far rather answer them than not.

Running late or need to move the appointment? Call your practice or message us on WhatsApp, we would rather rearrange than have you rush.

What happens at your appointment

  1. A proper welcomeWe show you around, and you meet the person looking after you.
  2. Your historyFull medical and social history, and how it links to your dental health. Tell us about any cosmetic concerns here too.
  3. The examinationTeeth and gums checked thoroughly for decay and disease.
  4. ScreeningOral cancer screening, plus a check of your jaw function and the glands outside your mouth.
  5. X-rays if neededOnly where we cannot see, between teeth, under fillings and the bone supporting your teeth.
  6. Your planWe take time to talk through what we found, and you leave with a written personal treatment plan and a price.

Nothing is done on the day that you have not agreed to. You decide in your own time.

Questions people ask before their first visit

I have not been to a dentist in years

Very common here, and not something to be embarrassed about. We see it every week and nobody will lecture you.

Will I need x-rays?

Only where we cannot see, between the teeth, under fillings and around the bone supporting your teeth. We will explain why before we take any.

Can I spread the cost of treatment?

Yes. Treatment can be staged over time and paid monthly. Ask at your appointment and we will set it out in writing.

What if I am in pain before my appointment?

Call your practice. We keep emergency slots and can assess urgently from £62.

Is there a membership plan?

Yes. It covers your routine examinations and x-rays and gives discounts on most treatment. We will talk you through it after your first visit.

Can I bring someone with me?

Of course. Plenty of patients bring a partner, friend or family member, especially for a first appointment.
