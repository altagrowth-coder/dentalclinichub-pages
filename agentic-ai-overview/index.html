What the agent does

Six Jobs The Agent Does Across Your Group

Agentic means it does the work, then hands a person anything that needs judgement. Here is what that looks like at every site.

01

Works every open plan

Scores each open treatment plan every night, chases at the right moment and stops the second a patient replies. Each plan ends in a yes or a clear no.

02

Answers enquiries in minutes

Evening and weekend enquiries get a reply the same evening, at every site, in your practice's voice.

03

Briefs each site every morning

Your managers open one short list: who to call, who is ready to book and who needs a clinician.

04

Shows the whole group on one screen

Acceptance, plan value and follow up by site and by clinician. You see which site to copy and which to coach.

05

Wakes up dormant patients

Finds patients who went quiet and brings them back with a reason to return.

06

Follows your rules

Your team sets the limits. The agent escalates anything clinical, financial or sensitive to a person.
