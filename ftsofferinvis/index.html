Your questions, answered

Why are Invisalign prices increasing?

Upcoming HMRC VAT rule changes mean the cost of providing Invisalign treatment increases from September. This is an industry-wide change, not a Face Teeth Smile decision.

How do I keep my current price?

Simply begin your treatment before the changes take effect in September. Once you’ve started, your pricing is fixed for your whole treatment plan.

I already had a consultation — do I need another one?

No. If you already have your treatment plan, just book in or contact us and we’ll get you started at your existing quoted price before the deadline.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes — flexible finance is available, with Invisalign from only £35 per month*. We’ll go through your options at the practice.

What happens at the free consultation?

We take a 3D digital scan of your teeth and show you a before-and-after simulation of your future smile, then talk through your bespoke plan, timings and pricing. It’s completely no-obligation.
