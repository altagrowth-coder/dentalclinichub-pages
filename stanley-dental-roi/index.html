Where these numbers come from

  • Google cost per enquiry: UK dental campaign benchmarks, Q3 2025 (Dental Design Marketing). Emergency £3.71 a click at 10.5% conversion, Invisalign £5.76 at 5.8%, implants £3.94 at 3.4%.
  • Meta cost per enquiry: UK dental ranges for 2026 (Upscale). Invisalign £25 to £60, single implants £40 to £100. Smaller towns often come in cheaper than this.
  • Booking rate: Meta enquiries book at around 10% on average. Our clinics book 20% to 25% with instant replies and steady WhatsApp and email follow-up. Google enquiries carry higher intent.
  • Acceptance: 42% average case acceptance across UK practices (Henry Schein One, 2026).
  • Fees start from local published prices (clear aligners from £3,200, implants from £2,900). Replace them with your own fees.
  • This is a planning model, not a guarantee. Results depend on your offer, your diary and how quickly the team follows up.
