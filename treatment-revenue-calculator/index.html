Where the Difference Comes From

Top decile clinics do not present more plans. They follow up better. The gap usually closes through four habits.

  • Every patient leaves with a clear plan they can read at home, with phases, prices and what happens next.
  • Monthly payment options shown up front, with a soft credit check that has no impact on the patient's score.
  • Follow-up that never stops at one call: WhatsApp, email and phone over weeks, not days.
  • Acceptance tracked by clinician and site, so the team can see what is working.
