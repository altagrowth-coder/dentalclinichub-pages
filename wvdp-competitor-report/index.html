What a winning first call looks like

The gap between these four practices is not answering speed. It is what happens after "Good morning, how can I help?" This is the six-step call a trained treatment coordinator runs, and how the competitors measured up.

  1. Answer fast, day or nightWithin a few rings, including evenings and weekends when ad enquiries peak.All four in the day. None out of hours.
  2. Build rapportUse the patient's name, show genuine interest, make them feel looked after.No.22 did this well. Raby Road did not.
  3. QualifyWhat treatment, what is bothering them, budget, timing, have they had other quotes.Primo and Raby Road skipped it.
  4. Explain the next step and the moneyConsultation format, fee or deposit, and finance options in plain numbers.Not recorded at any practice.
  5. Book while they are on the lineOffer two specific slots and confirm the booking there and then.Primo did. Raby Road did not try.
  6. Follow upConfirmation text, reminders and a call-back if they did not book, so nobody drifts to a competitor.The next layer we can test.

Method and sources

How This Was Put Together

  • Websites: every page listed was loaded in a real browser on 8 Oct 2026. Prices, hours and offers are copied from the live pages; screenshots are unedited captures. Tracking tools were identified from the network requests each page makes.
  • Meta ads: Meta Ad Library, United Kingdom, all ad types, exact-phrase search on each practice name, filtered to the practice's own page. "Live" means the Ad Library showed the ad as active on 8 Oct 2026.
  • Google ads: Google Ads Transparency Centre, United Kingdom, searched by each practice's website domain. No ads were found for wvdp.co.uk or no22dental.co.uk.
  • Spend estimates: Meta and Google do not publish spend for health ads. Monthly ranges are modelled from the number of live creatives and campaigns, how long they have run, impression flags shown by Meta, and typical UK local dental budgets (Meta £10 to £35 per day per active campaign; Google local search £2 to £6 per click). Treat them as order-of-magnitude estimates, not invoices.
  • Mystery shop: one call to each practice's main published number on Monday 12 October 2026, during opening hours, by an AltaDent team member posing as a new private patient. Results reflect a single call each; where something was not observed it is marked "not recorded" rather than assumed.
  • Reviews: Google ratings taken from Google ads, practice review widgets and review aggregators; where sources differ a range is shown.
